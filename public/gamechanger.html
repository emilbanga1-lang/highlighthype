For Parents

Your clip is already filmed.
Get it into the Hype.

If your team streams on GameChanger, your athlete's best plays are already clipped and waiting in the app. Moving one to HighlightHype takes about a minute — here's exactly how.

Step 1 — Save the clip from GameChanger

GameChanger automatically clips plays from every game that was streamed and scored.

  1. Open your team in GameChanger and go to your player's page.
  2. Tap the Video Clips tab. You can filter by batting, fielding, or pitching to find the play faster.
  3. On the clip, tap the ⋯ More icon, then Share Clip.
  4. Give it about 45 seconds — GameChanger prepares the clip (one at a time), then a share sheet pops up.
  5. Tap Save Video. The clip is now in your camera roll.
Another way in: open the game from the Schedule tab, find the play in the Play-by-Play, tap the clip, then tap the white share button in the corner of the player — same share sheet, same Save Video.
On Android? GameChanger saves to Google Photos or Google Drive instead of your gallery. Save it there — you'll pick it from there in Step 2.

Step 2 — Post it on HighlightHype

Straight from your camera roll — no editing, no trimming.

  1. Tap the button below, then tap Upload a Hype.
  2. Choose the clip you just saved.
  3. Pick your athlete, name the play, and confirm you're the parent or guardian.
  4. Hit submit. Every play is reviewed by a real person — then it's live on the board and in the running for Hype of the Month.
Upload a Hype

No clips on your player's page?

Clips are only created when the game was both streamed and scored in GameChanger — if nobody streamed it, there are no clips to grab. Highlight clips are free for team staff; parents may need a GameChanger subscription to download them. Your coach or scorekeeper can tell you which games were streamed.

Filmed it yourself instead?

Even easier — skip Step 1. Any clip in your camera roll can go straight in through Upload a Hype.
