How it works

Post. Share. Get hyped.

Three steps, about a minute. If you can send a clip to the group chat, you already know how this works.

01

Post your play

Film it, or find it in your camera roll. Video first — upload the clip, then add the sport and team. The moment is the content; no editing needed.

02

Share your link

Every approved play gets its own link. Send it to family, drop it in the group chat — that's where the votes come from.

03

Get hyped

One vote per person, per play, per month. Most votes this month takes Hype of the Month. All-time votes climb to the Top 10 Hall of Fame.

Reviewed before it's live. Every upload is checked by a person before it reaches the board — nothing posts automatically.
